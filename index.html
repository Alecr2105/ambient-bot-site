Ambient Bot

Publishing tool for a nature ambience channel filmed in Costa Rica.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What this is

Ambient Bot is a private, internal tool used by a single channel owner to publish long-form ambient videos (rain, rainforest, rivers, waterfalls and similar nature soundscapes) on the owner's own YouTube channel. All footage is filmed by the channel owner in Costa Rica.

Channel: Costa Rica Ambience

How it uses YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos, set their title, description, tags and thumbnail, schedule publication, and add videos to the owner's playlists. It only acts on the channel of the person who authorizes it. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's data.
